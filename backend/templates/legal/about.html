{% trans "Why Preceptly?" %}

{% trans "Working as a freelance tutor at two institutes, organizing lessons, contracts, and invoices across different tools quickly became overwhelming." %}

{% trans "Preceptly was built to have everything in one place — scheduling, contracts, invoices, and income — clearly arranged and without the chaos." %}

{% trans "The developer" %}

{% trans "Preceptly is developed and used by Andreas Eirich, a freelance web developer from Ankum, Germany." %}

{% trans "He works as a tutor on a fee basis at TutorSpace and Abacus, and developed Preceptly because no suitable tool existed for his own tutoring work." %}

{% trans "The goal is to offer it to other tutors in the future. Under andicode he runs a web development business; further projects are listed at andicode.de." %}

{% trans "Technology" %}

{% trans "Preceptly is built on Django 5 and Python 3.12, backed by a PostgreSQL database and hosted on Railway." %}

{% trans "All data is hosted on Railway (Railway Corp., USA) and processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO). Data transfers to the USA are safeguarded by EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)." %}

{% trans "The technology stack was chosen for its maturity, security track record, and long-term maintainability." %}
